Brick Screen Residence

Residential • Dhaka, Bangladesh • 2025 • Completed

Specifications

Typology: Single-Family Residence

Location: Dhaka, Bangladesh

Year: 2025

Status: Built

Site Area: 3,600 sq.ft.

Built Area: 5,200 sq.ft.

Key Materials: Local Terracotta Brick, Fair-Face Concrete, Teak

Concept: Perforated Jali & Passive Climate Envelope

Design Narrative

Nestled within a residential enclave of Dhaka, the Brick Screen Residence pays homage to Bengal's millenia-old brickmaking traditions while executing a contemporary, disciplined architectural form. The primary southern facade features an intricate double-skin terracotta jali screen.

This screen acts as a breathable environmental buffer: filtering intense tropical afternoon sun, casting dancing geometric shadows across the interior terrazzo floors, and inducing passive air movement even on windless humid days.

Behind the porous brick veil, deep recessed balconies and landscaped courtyards create intimate private retreats that remain shielded from the adjacent street without shutting out the neighborhood life.
