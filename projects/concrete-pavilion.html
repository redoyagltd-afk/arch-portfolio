The Concrete Pavilion

Residential • Dhaka, Bangladesh • 2025 • Completed

Specifications

Typology: Residential / Student Shelter

Location: Dhaka, Bangladesh

Year: 2025

Status: Built

Key Materials: Board-marked Concrete, Terracotta, Teak

Concept: Pitched Roofs & Fluid Flight Geometry

Design Narrative

This project explores the intersection of raw materiality and lightweight, dynamic forms. Inspired by traditional regional roof profiles and the fluid contours of birds in flight, the massing transitions smoothly from ground-level concrete stability into soaring open vistas.

Large glass apertures puncture the brutalist concrete form, allowing natural light to wash across the interior surfaces throughout the day, creating an ever-shifting play of shadows and connecting the occupants directly with changing daylight.

Terracotta screening filters direct tropical solar radiation while maintaining continuous cross-ventilation, balancing private refuge with climatic responsiveness.
