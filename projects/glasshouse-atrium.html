Glasshouse Atrium

Commercial • Dhaka, Bangladesh • 2024 • Completed

Specifications

Typology: Commercial / Cultural Space

Location: Dhaka, Bangladesh

Year: 2024

Status: Built

Site Area: 6,800 sq.ft.

Built Area: 18,500 sq.ft.

Key Materials: Structural Steel, Low-E Glass, Cast Concrete

Concept: Vertical Transparency & Urban Atrium

Design Narrative

Positioned at a bustling urban intersection in Dhaka, Glasshouse Atrium explores how vertical transparency can create civic porosity within dense commercial districts. The building steps back from the street line, offering a public plaza before ascending into a dramatic vertical atrium.

Cantilevered glass volumes project outward from a rugged concrete spine, modulating natural light throughout the interior floors. The central light well acts as a natural thermal chimney, drawing warm air upward and reducing mechanical cooling demands.

By night, the structure acts as an urban lantern, revealing its internal staircases, mezzanine galleries, and dynamic spatial rhythm to passersby on the street below.
